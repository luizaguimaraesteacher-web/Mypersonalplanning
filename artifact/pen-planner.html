        Only seeing "Busy" for a calendar?

        That calendar is shared with your connected Google account as free/busy only. To see the event names and details:

        1. Sign in to Google Calendar on a computer with the account that owns that calendar.
        2. Next to the calendar: ⋮ → Settings and sharing → Share with specific people.
        3. Find the account connected to Claude and change See only free/busy to See all event details (or Make changes to events). If it isn't listed, add it.
        4. Work or school account? If See all event details is greyed out, the organisation's admin only allows free/busy sharing outside it: ask them, or share from a personal calendar instead.
        5. Come back and tap ↻. It can take a few minutes to change.

              Add another calendar

              1. Open Google Calendar on a computer, signed in to the account that has the calendar (for example your personal Gmail).
              2. Next to the calendar, choose Settings and sharing → Share with specific people → Add people, add the Google account connected to Claude, and give it See all event details (or Make changes to edit from the planner).
              3. Copy the Calendar ID from Integrate calendar (for your main calendar it's just your email address) and paste it here.
