    Morning routine

    The steps you want to do every day, in order.

      Connect Google Calendar

      The planner shows each day's events from your Google Calendar. You set it up once. It's easier on a computer, and then you paste the link here on your iPad.

      2. Go to script.google.com and sign in. A new project opens. Delete the text that's there and paste the script.
      3. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
      4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
      5. Click Authorize access and choose your account. Google warns that the app isn't verified, because you made it yourself: click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.
      6. Copy the Web app URL and paste it below.

      Keep this link private: anyone who has it can see your calendar events. It only reads your calendar; it can't change anything.
